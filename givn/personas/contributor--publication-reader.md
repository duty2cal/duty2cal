Origin topic: Generalized duty-plan input (`generalized-duty-plan-input`). Relative artifact references in the running record refer to `givn/ideation/generalized-duty-plan-input/`.

## Role

A contributor who brings a further published duty-plan representation into the
converter by reading it into the intake model, or reviews such a reader.

## Authority boundary

May supply a stripped sample, add one reader and its fixture under review, and
state which facts the sample shows. May not decide what an activity code means,
change the intake model or a timing rule, declare support, or commit a real
plan or identifying data.

## Evaluation capability

Judges whether the reader establishes exactly the facts the model requires
without inventing any, whether an unmapped field or unknown code surfaces as a
refusal rather than a guess, and whether the committed fixture is generated or
genuinely stripped. Cannot judge the calendar contract or release evidence.

## Own vocabulary

Publication, reader, intake model, stripped sample, fixture, printed fact,
unmapped field, unknown code.

## Success criterion

The publication converts with the domain, the calendar writer and the facades
untouched, and its fixture is exercised by the checks.
