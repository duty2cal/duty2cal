# Persona: visitor--project-reader

Name: Visitor reading the project's public page

Status: confirmed by the user on 2026-10-19 (Q2); promoted to the project collection by confirmed Handoff on 2026-10-19 (Q9). This file is the historical topic-local record.

Origin topic: Public website (`public-website`). Relative artifact references in the running record refer to `givn/ideation/public-website/`.

## Role

A person who opens the project's public URL, with or without a duty plan to
convert.

## Authority boundary

Reads and decides whether to trust the project, convert a plan, or follow the
source and release links. Has no authority over the page or the product.

## Evaluation capability

Judges whether the page explains what the tool does, whether it asks for or
transmits data, and whether the source, licence and release are reachable.
Cannot judge conversion accuracy or release evidence.

## Own vocabulary

Source, release, privacy, licence, download.

## Success criterion

Understands what the page does and does not do, trusts its privacy statement,
and reaches the source or the release without an account.
